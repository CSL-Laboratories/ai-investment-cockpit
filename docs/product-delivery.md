# Integrated product delivery

The first complete release is the product in [PRODUCT](../PRODUCT.md), with the
[operating contracts](product-operating-contracts.md),
[tax contract](product-tax-contract.md) and [acceptance contract](product-acceptance.md).
Adopted by Lucas under PD-048 on 2026-09-09, this plan supplies implementation order
under PLAN and explicit dispositions for conflicting older scope. RW-25 remains
documentation only. The slice names below are planning
labels, not unclaimed work-package IDs or permission to deploy/spend/run a pilot.

## Deliver connected behavior

Every implementation claim must identify a user-visible outcome, the existing
application paths it extends, the replaced path it retires, production reachability
evidence and required safety/platform gates. Claim one bounded package in REGISTER
before code. Split a slice into such packages when necessary; do not create one
unreviewable rewrite or leave duplicate production and test-only workflows alive.

| Sequence | Delivery slice | Concrete result and acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| A | Feasibility and evidence qualification | Compare a bounded strategy shortlist including current cash-merger; inspect actual useful/empty run artifacts, data populations and available model routes; define independent evaluation references, metrics and frozen evaluation protocols |
| B | Useful research through the real application | A core/discovered/submitted item reaches retained evidence, a cited case and delivered short update through `main.compose`; unknown/rejected cases explain what happened and what evidence would change it |
| C | Shared authority, executor and recovery contracts | Attributed actions on Discord and the host, bounded real model jobs, aggregate limits and durable holds; independently review the paper-autonomy and eligibility redesign before order-authority implementation |
| D | Integrated strategy-to-paper lifecycle | Activate qualified strategies through owner review, admit eligible symbols under approved rules, allocate shared cash and complete paper entry/monitoring/exit/reconciliation with cost/outcome evidence |
| E | Full tax and economic workflow | Supported intake facts reach versioned federal/California calculations, complete/scenario/incomplete results and economic reports; validate every selected account family and privacy policy |
| F | Release qualification and unattended pilot | Exact release candidate passes production replays, installed adapters, both operating systems, applicable independent evidence and at least 20 Linux market days plus required real workflow coverage |

B and C establish shared contracts early; D depends on A's strategy qualification and
C's reviewed authority. E can develop against the same evidence/ledger contracts while
D proceeds, but tax-dependent D actions wait for their qualified tax requirements.
F needs all selected launch capabilities, including rich input types and the full tax
engine. Early useful research is a delivered increment, not a redefinition of the MVP.

## A — bounded feasibility and trustworthy evaluation

At claim time freeze the shortlist, work/time/resource allowance, source acquisition
permissions, comparison criteria and stopping conditions. Initial comparison:
the current cash-merger strategy plus at most five materially distinct alternatives
within cash-funded, long-only US stocks/ETFs. Compare evidence attainability, as-of
integrity, data coverage/cost, execution and tax requirements, supported horizons,
testability, incremental portfolio exposure and cost-adjusted evaluation feasibility.
Do not select winners using the same outcomes later presented as held-out validation.

Deliver a versioned strategy contract and explicit qualify/watch/reject result for
each candidate. Choose two or three launch strategies only after the required evidence
exists and Lucas approves activation. Parameter changes do not inflate the count.
If fewer than two qualify, deliver useful research and identify the specific evidence
or scope decision needed; neither recurring empty runs nor lowering a gate completes
the release. Cash-merger must be compared but is not guaranteed a launch place.

Inspect a bounded sample of actual delivered operational cases and their raw packets,
plus empty/rejected runs. Classify acquisition, extraction, reasoning, eligibility,
delivery and scheduling failures separately. The September evidence screen and the
[feasibility review](research/gate-feasibility-and-value-2026-09-06.md) already show
that successful mechanics do not establish an attainable strategy evidence population.
Empty GET-only screens are recorded in `app/feasibility/screens.v1.json`
(RW-31/RW-32) as useful research; they do not lower the 30-identity cohort
gate or authorize labelling. The 2026-09-12 identity list is pinned from
an operator seal of summary hashes; raw SEC packets remain off-clone.
Further data collection needs its own authorized resource/source claim.

Freeze reference provenance, population selection, as-of boundaries, evaluation/tuning
separation, metrics, thresholds and ambiguity treatment before measuring results.
Include rejects and low-tier dismissals. Publish per-population coverage and an
unverified outcome when references are insufficient. New strategy-specific statistical
thresholds are outputs of this claimed design work; they cannot remain unset when
its evaluation starts, be inferred from model confidence or be tuned after outcomes.

Qualify model judges against independently established source-grounded references.
Measure unsupported claims, missing facts and missed escalation, including confident
early rejections. Different model names do not establish independent truth. Automated
routine scoring is the target; existing independent label requirements retain the
disposition below until a qualified replacement has been reviewed.

## B–E — implementation boundaries and observable exits

**B:** extend the existing acquisition/raw-store/workflow/status/Discord composition.
Introduce typed evidence and case contracts with one durable trace. Deliver one real
source-to-case update early, then cover every selected input category and its failures.
Private document intake needs local extraction or explicitly permitted disclosure.
Retire or redirect parallel scheduling/reporting paths so deployed and tested entry
points use the same graph. Check actual useful content and citations, not only delivery.

**C:** implement stable principals and per-action grants, resource reservations, bounded
job receipts and durable recovery in separately bounded claims. Test the same denied
and permitted actions across surfaces. The paper-autonomy design must explicitly state
standing approval semantics, allowable model jobs, expiry/revocation and final gateway
verification. Threat-model stale/replayed approvals, confused principals, model/tool
escape, changed evidence, concurrent cap allocation, failure and restart. Obtain the
required adversarial review before adopting authority changes. Broker tools and
credentials remain outside every model and MCP execution context.

**D:** reuse and extend `PaperExecutionService`, the deterministic controls and
`OrderGateway`; do not create a second execution path for the new strategies. Add
strategy contracts, deterministic evidence/eligibility verification and one portfolio
allocator. Exercise partial fills, cancellation races, corporate actions, settlement,
stale decisions, unknown submissions and restart reconciliation. A strategy activation
review includes applicable empirical evidence and scope; a green code suite alone
does not make it executable. Expand from the first integrated eligible strategy to
the full two-or-three suite before calling the MVP complete.

**E:** implement the entire selected tax contract, including account/year applicability,
outside-account context, private input handling and independent expected results.
Demonstrate taxpayer-complete reference calculations as well as the selected explicit
paper-scenario policy. Integrate tax effects and costs into contribution-adjusted
economic reporting, and tax-dependent refusals into the same action controls. Ordinary
launch profiles returning only "incomplete" cannot satisfy full tax capability.

## F — release and pilot manifest

Before the pilot, freeze the candidate revision/artifact, dependency and adapter
versions, active strategy/policy versions, tax rule sets, source/evaluation populations,
required scenarios, metrics/thresholds, baseline and cost assumptions, operating caps,
observation window and continuation/reset rules. Configuration and credentials remain
private; the manifest uses redacted identifiers/hashes where appropriate.

Pilot reset rule: a material change to decisions, execution, safety, required tax
calculations or evaluated behavior starts a new candidate observation window. A
nonbehavioral correction may continue only with recorded impact evidence under the
predeclared policy. Retain previous observations with their original revisions. A
dependency outage is measured and investigated; it is not erased from reliability
results by renaming the run. Days without required observation cannot be counted as
fully observed market days. Long-lived positions or missing coverage extend the pilot.

Required evidence includes the four DEC-09 local cells, operational installation and
recovery on Windows and Linux, named installed route/Discord/broker checks and the
primary Linux pilot of at least 20 market days. Use the real paper-broker path for
the required lifecycle demonstration; replays cover rare faults but cannot replace
actual broker conformance. No otherwise-ineligible trade may be placed to fill a test
cell. Record per-strategy coverage and keep unmet evidence as a release blocker.

The Cloud Devin swarm may prepare replay fixtures and review packages. It does
**not** run the 20 market days, live smoke, or broker-facing soaks. Those remain
owner-operated (Grok Bot or another service Lucas chooses). See
[research/agent-swarm-cloud-host-2026-09-11.md](research/agent-swarm-cloud-host-2026-09-11.md).

The release review contains useful research examples and measured errors, routing and
quota/cost results, honest strategy outcomes, full tax coverage, incident/recovery
evidence, remaining limitations and installation/rollback plans. Lucas approves the
software release; its installation does not grant new strategy, cap or live authority.

## After the MVP

Keep the strategy candidate queue active within approved research caps. Deliver
monthly expansion summaries and candidate-ready notifications, evaluate incremental
net contribution and shared exposures, and seek owner activation of qualified additions.
Add Recursion after real executor conformance passes the shared contract; a fixture
or architecture resemblance is insufficient. Further jurisdictions, special retirement
cases and instrument scopes require verified capability additions and applicable
approvals. Live trading remains a separate progression governed by existing gates.

# Release evidence and acceptance proposal

**Disposition:** proposed technical contract for Round 8. The 20-market-day pilot
minimum and monthly expansion reporting are selected under PD-039/PD-040. Other
details below remain proposals for final review. Owner directions are recorded in
[product-planning](product-planning.md); [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) is still a candidate.
This document specifies evidence to obtain, not tests or deployment trials already run.

## What completion must demonstrate

The selected release is an unattended, standalone research-to-paper application with
two or three owner-activated strategies, cited research, all selected input categories,
dynamic model routing, authorized interaction and complete position management.
It includes full investment-tax calculations for its explicitly supported context.
It supports Windows and Linux, with the primary pilot on Lucas's Linux box. Research
quality evaluation targets full automation. Results can be unfavorable and must remain
visible; profitability is not required for the working-product completion declaration.

The [operating contracts](product-operating-contracts.md) define the proposed shared
workflow, permissions, input manifest and recovery behavior. The
[delivery plan](product-delivery.md) owns proposed implementation slices and explicit
dispositions for current evidence and authority requirements.

The following evidence must connect to the actual application and its release revision.
A passing unit suite, successful subprocess exit or empty daily screen cannot substitute
for useful output and observed paper operation. No minimum order count authorizes
manufactured trades or bypasses a strategy gate.

## Evidence layers

| Layer | Required evidence | What it establishes |
|---|---|---|
| Production composition | Controlled replay through the actual composition and application actions, with external leaves substituted | Internal integration, deterministic policy and recovery behavior; no claim of real provider or broker conformance |
| Installed adapters | Contract checks against each admitted CLI/model route, delivery surface and paper-broker adapter | Behavior of the named versions, interfaces and account routes; a fake subprocess alone cannot certify them |
| Deployed operation | Unattended pilot with configured feeds, qualifying model routes, Discord and real paper-broker execution | Delivery, lifecycle and recovery on the deployed candidate; observed gaps remain gaps |
| Quality and economics | Source-grounded reference evaluation, actual paper outcomes and predeclared comparisons/costs | Measured usefulness, errors, missed cases and strategy performance within the observed population |

The existing native Windows and Ubuntu/WSL2 × Python 3.11/3.12 matrix remains mandatory.
Both operating systems also need installation, credential access, startup, shutdown,
restart and diagnostics acceptance. Pilot work does not replace the local matrix.

## Workflow coverage

| Product behavior | Required positive and failure evidence |
|---|---|
| Core coverage, discovery and human intake | Tickers, links, ideas, questions, documents, screenshots and images reach retained inputs and an attributable case; unsupported/corrupt/ambiguous inputs receive a useful explanation |
| Evidence and research | Material claims link to retained sources and ingestion methods; missing or conflicting evidence produces an explicit unknown/watch outcome with a revisit trigger |
| Research value | Brief updates lead to detailed cases; unchanged evidence avoids redundant work; empty runs report coverage, rejected inputs, missing evidence and next actions rather than claiming success from completion alone |
| Strategy reasoning | Each of the two or three distinct strategies has applicable evidence, evaluated behavior and owner activation; engine-selected horizons stay within the activated contract |
| Symbol admission | Automatic admission obeys owner-approved eligibility rules and binds required evidence; research alone, expired eligibility and failed rechecks cannot authorize an order |
| Model routing | Qualifying routes are selected within aggregate limits; record capability, diversity and quota considerations, early dismissals, escalation decisions and fallback reasons |
| Autonomous paper lifecycle | Eligible entries, monitoring, permitted changes/cancellations, exits and reconciliation pass through the actual order path; partial fills and uncertain submissions do not create duplicate orders |
| Failures and recovery | Missing-input scenarios block affected increased exposure, preserve monitoring and permit only qualifying protective actions; checked transient recovery cannot clear human/integrity/authority holds |
| Identity and releases | Different users receive only granted actions across surfaces; audit binds actor and policy; isolated agent development cannot install a new release without owner approval |
| Improvement loop | A recorded failure or authorized request produces a bounded isolated engineering job and reviewable tested revision or explained unresolved result; neither the running engine nor a decision job gains development or installation authority |
| Economics and expansion | Contributions are distinct from P&L; costs and losses remain visible; expansion queue and selected reminders lead to review rather than automatic activation |
| Full investment-tax calculations | Supported jurisdiction/account/year cases match independent reference results using verified rules and complete inputs; missing context yields an explicit incomplete result rather than invented liability |

Offline replays must include a healthy positive control and injected faults that fail
when essential production wiring or controls are removed. Use the actual composition
root; do not create a second workflow only for tests. Recorded-source replays need
identified provenance and as-of boundaries. Synthetic cases are useful for fault
coverage but cannot become prospective market observations or independent labels.

The real paper pilot must demonstrate a completed eligible position lifecycle. Rare
or longer strategy paths may also require replay coverage and longer observation.
Report coverage per strategy; replay alone cannot prove that a broker-side path works.
If a required real path has not occurred or cannot be exercised under its approved
contract, acceptance remains incomplete. Do not trigger an otherwise-ineligible order
just to obtain the missing demonstration.

## Automated evaluation qualification

Routine quality evaluation should not require a daily operator or human sample review.
Combine deterministic checks and source-grounded reference cases with model judges only
where their own errors and limits have been measured. Keep reference/evaluation examples
separate from tuning examples and include rejects, early dismissals, refusals, empty runs
and escalations. Independent model agreement alone is not a reference truth label.

Record factual support, material omissions, extraction errors, useful case outcomes,
coverage/abstention, missed escalation, latency, cost and quota use. A high score on a
small easy subset cannot conceal the unverified remainder. Unsupported claims and
unresolved judgments stay visible; schema validity does not establish semantic truth.

Before a release evaluation begins, freeze its manifest: candidate revision, source
population and hashes, as-of rules, reference-generation method, evaluator versions,
sampling plan, metric definitions, thresholds, required coverage and failure policy.
Strategy baselines, costs, contribution assumptions and observation rules are frozen
before outcomes are inspected. Material changes require an explicit invalidation or
new evaluation; do not tune the pass line after seeing results.

The delivery plan explicitly retains current RW-11 and merger independent-label
requirements while proposing automated repeat evaluation over qualified references.
Its first slice must qualify evaluators and any replacement reference method before
use, with evidence of adequacy and independence for each replaced claim. Existing
labels and witness gates are not satisfied by model agreement. Missing reference
quality or an unqualified evaluator means unverified evidence, not an automatic PASS.

## Economic baseline and reports

Use $2,000 as a provisional paper balance derived from Lucas's likely minimum-transfer
intention. The owner-stated $210/month covers ChatGPT Pro and OpenCode Go; it is not a
verified price quote or the total of every possible operating cost. Preserve that full
expense-coverage target while separately identifying any shared-use allocation.

Reports separate starting capital, external contributions/withdrawals, realized P&L,
unrealized P&L, trading costs, recurring engine costs, benchmark results and drawdown.
Disclose whether costs are deducted in the simulated cash ledger or in the economic
comparison; never deduct them twice. Compare portfolios with matched external cash
flows. A recurring contribution cannot be counted as investment gain.

At $2,000, the stated subscriptions alone require $210 / $2,000 = 10.5% per month
after trading costs to be covered at that static balance. This is an arithmetic hurdle,
not expected performance. Other costs, taxes and additional growth are separate.
The owner reports $500/month of student funding for the coming year but has not selected
a contribution percentage; do not assume that funding is all investable or indefinite.
Contribution scenarios require explicit amounts and timing. Show a reference scenario
without assumed future contributions and preserve losses and periods below the target.

## Full investment-tax calculation scope

PD-042 makes the calculation engine a first-release requirement. A ledger and assumed
tax haircut alone cannot satisfy it. PD-043 through PD-045 select US federal and
California first, with extensible verified rules, for taxable brokerage, Traditional/
Roth IRAs and 401(k), 403(b), governmental 457(b) accounts. Tax support is independent
of broker account-trading support. The [tax contract](product-tax-contract.md) maps
this coverage to sources, required transaction/outside-account context, calculation
results and reference validation. Qualify the applicable pilot tax year explicitly.

Recommended design uses deterministic calculations with versioned official rules,
source citations and inspectable intermediate values. Distinguish an input-complete
calculation from a paper scenario estimate, and surface unsupported years, missing
data or ambiguous treatment explicitly. Tax-derived restrictions need their own
validated applicability and authority. Calculation completeness does not imply full
personal-return preparation, filing or submission authority.

PD-046 permits explicit paper scenarios with missing personal context, while blocking
actions whose required tax restrictions remain unverified. PD-047 keeps private
documents local by default and allows only minimum approved, redacted facts on external
model routes unless the owner grants broader scoped access. Acceptance must verify
these policies through intake, extraction, handoffs and fallbacks, including a refusal
when the only available provider lacks the required disclosure permission.

## Selected pilot and expansion cadence

**PD-039:** at least 20 market days on the primary Linux deployment plus required
workflow/scenario coverage and all applicable strategy-evidence gates. This is an
operational observation minimum, not a claim that 20 days establishes financial
advantage. Longer lifecycles or evidence gaps can extend it. Before starting, specify
candidate revisions, maintenance windows, allowed interruptions and which changes reset
or invalidate observations. An elapsed window alone cannot pass the pilot.

**PD-040:** a monthly strategy-expansion summary and candidate-ready notifications.
Report progress, missing evidence and the next step; activation remains owner-approved.
Reminders do not require a daily review or increase the research budget automatically.
The first monthly summary must also be covered in acceptance, even if the market-day
minimum ends before its scheduled delivery; a controlled clock can verify scheduling,
while observed delivery remains separately identified. Suppress duplicate notifications
for unchanged candidate readiness. Exact scheduling and retry rules need specification.

## Review and implementation qualification

The operating, tax and delivery contracts now supply the proposed permission/revocation
matrix, supported intake formats/limits, configuration requirements, tax coverage,
bounded feasibility/evaluator work and delivery slices. Their technical defaults and
reconciliation dispositions await final owner review alongside PRODUCT. Numeric
operating caps must be owner-configured before autonomous operation. Feasibility work
must establish and freeze new strategy/evaluator thresholds before measuring outcomes.
These are explicit prerequisites to implementation/pilot acceptance; this documentation
package claims no current empirical gate has passed.

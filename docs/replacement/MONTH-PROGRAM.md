# Candidate: Cockpit replacement month program

The program is ordered by contract dependency, not calendar optimism. Research and
review may overlap; implementation consumes only accepted contract fragments.

## Outcome order

### O1

**Successor product and economic contract:** freeze first-user, markets,
   measurable value, month slice, non-goals and evidence/release semantics.
### O2

**Multi-market evidence and replay plane:** consumes O1 source and value
   clauses; freezes point-in-time data, provenance, replay and market-semantic input.
### O3

**Instrument, portfolio and accounting semantics:** consumes O1 and O2;
   freezes stocks/event contracts, cash, collateral, fees, fills, resolution,
   positions, attribution, reconciliation and recovery truth.
### O4

**Strategy research and economic qualification laboratory:** consumes O1 to
   O3; freezes baselines, tuning/evaluation split, cost/yield/risk measures and
   promotion protocols.
### O5

**Provider-neutral decision laboratory:** consumes O1, O2 and O4; compares
   installed decision routes without granting order authority.
### O6

**Configurable authority, execution and recovery:** consumes O1, O3, O4 and
   O5 evidence; freezes non-widening compliance profiles, action semantics,
   reconciliation, intervention and staged authorization. It implements separate A
   proposal-only, B model-assisted and C bounded-direct profiles over one independent
   enforcement layer, with A as the qualification reference and C only as the
   owner-selected future target. It binds scope, expiry, cumulative budgets,
   revocation, persistent stops, authoritative restart reconciliation and
   capability-specific evidence from the completed Parallel authority brief.
### O7

**GUI and CLI operator product:** consumes O1, O2, O3 and O6 public service
   contracts; exposes evidence, strategies, positions, budgets, refusals, health,
   interventions and audit without a privileged order path.
### O8

**Migration and performance frontier:** consumes frozen fragments from O2 to
   O7; classifies legacy components and measures matched end-to-end candidates before
   selecting replacement language, storage or topology.
### O9

**Qualification, packaging and release evidence:** consumes all accepted
   outcome contracts; binds exact-candidate qualification, packaging, recovery,
   compatibility and release evidence.

No dependency may point to a later outcome. Related evidence may inform an earlier
contract without becoming a hard reverse dependency.

Dependencies bind accepted contract fragments, not completion of a whole outcome.
Every consumed fragment needs its identifier, version or digest, owner, acceptance
criterion and receipt status. Informational feedback does not create a hard edge.

## Split-phase checkpoints that prevent cycles

- **O2/O3:** freeze a minimal canonical identity, currency, clock and source envelope
  before ingestion work. O3 may enrich accounting semantics only through a versioned
  compatibility amendment; it may not silently change already admitted O2 evidence.
- **O4/O5:** freeze evaluation populations, baselines, metrics and thresholds before
  observing provider results. O5 consumes that protocol; no provider result may
  rewrite it.
- **O6/O7:** O6 first freezes a non-privileged service/intervention contract that a
  CLI and GUI can share. Final operator acceptance can follow O7; neither surface is
  required to invent or widen O6 authority.
- **O8 phase A:** freeze interfaces, correctness and safety floors, a matched
  comparison protocol, and smallest candidate experiments before affected
  implementation. **O8 phase B** consumes minimal matched O2–O7 candidates for a
  human architecture/migration decision. No outcome waits for the final migration
  decision while also producing its evidence.
- **O9 phase A:** freeze capability-scoped qualification cells, exact-candidate
  identity and evidence protocols before runs. **O9 phase B** consumes final evidence
  and prepares a release decision. Evidence producers do not wait for release
  qualification, and offline/demo/paper testing uses current non-live authority
  rather than depending on a future live-enablement decision.

## Rolling checkpoints

- **C0 Baseline reconciled:** repository, Linear, audit and owner decisions agree on
  current versus historical state.
- **C1 Contract candidates:** O1 to O3 and issue/evidence rules are reviewable.
- **C2 Human receipts:** exact foundational digests are accepted or affected lines
  remain visibly blocked.
- **C3 Thin vertical slice:** selected O2 to O7 fragments compose through shared
  identity, provenance, accounting, authority and recovery semantics.
- **C4 Qualification:** exact candidates run declared replay, simulation, demo or
  paper and failure protocols; unknowns remain visible.
- **C5 Release decision:** O9 reports maturity per capability; a human decides scope.

## Month completion

Completion means each outcome has an evidence-backed maturity tier, every
consequential decision has a receipt or visible block, implemented capabilities point
to accepted contract digests, and deferred work has named dependencies. It does not
mean every outcome is implemented, profitable, safe, eligible for live use or
release-qualified.
